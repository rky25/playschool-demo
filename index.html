A Day at Little Sprouts

Your Child's Joyful Daily Routine

Here is exactly what a typical day looks like. No guesswork — you'll know every moment your child is safe, happy, and learning.

☀️
09:00 AM

Warm Welcome & Free Play

Greeted by name at the gate. Children settle in with soft toys, building blocks, or drawing — at their own pace.

🎵
09:30 AM

Circle Time, Rhymes & Morning Prayer

Everyone gathers on the rainbow mat for action songs, greetings, and a cheerful start to the day together.

🧩
10:15 AM

Sensory Table / Montessori Stations

Hands-on learning — pouring, sorting, threading, sand texture exploration, and puzzles that build fine motor grip.

🍎
11:00 AM

Handwashing & Healthy Snack Time

Supervised handwashing routine. Children eat their home-packed tiffin together while learning table manners.

📚
11:30 AM

Phonics, Storytelling & Puppet Theatre

Age-appropriate letter sounds, picture books, and puppet shows that build vocabulary and listening comprehension.

⛹️
12:15 PM

Gross Motor Outdoor / Sandpit Play

Running, climbing, balancing, and free play in the secure outdoor area. Builds strength, coordination, and happy memories.

👋
12:45 PM

Pack-up & Cheerful Goodbye Circle

Children sing a farewell song, collect their bags, and are handed over to verified parents/guardians at the gate.
